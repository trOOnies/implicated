Help

🎧 What is implicated?

A personal library of the artists, albums and songs you like. Everything you add is part of the library straight away; listening to it is optional.

🗺️ Pages

📊 Overview
Click the implicated logo at the top left to come here: totals, latest releases and charts of your library. Play history and favorites show up here once you have some. Its lists show 10 records at most; See more... opens the rest on the Artists, Albums or Songs page, already filtered.
🎤 Artists
Every artist with their genres, over a grey picture of them when they have one, a page at a time; filter them by name, origin, genre or language, or show only favorites or listen later. The Add artist form can mark a new artist as a favorite or to listen later and rate it right away; the year it formed cannot be in the future. Open one to see its line-up and discography, edit it (also to write your own notes, which imports leave alone), or add albums.
💿 Albums
All albums with their covers and how many of their songs have lyrics, a page at a time, your favorites first (or Newest first); the ♡ on a cover marks one. Open one for its tracklist, rating and notes; songs without lyrics are marked missing lyrics, and songs with no words to have (tick No lyrics when adding or editing them) no lyrics. Those do not count against the album's lyrics percentage. The CD and Vinyl selectors on an album page (and in an artist's discography) say whether you want or own it on each format; filter the albums by Wanted (CD), Owned (CD), Wanted (Vinyl) or Owned (Vinyl), favorites, listen later, the ones with songs still missing lyrics, or the ones with no own cover: albums marked no cover (the ♪ placeholder shows instead) or shared cover (the same image as another album, like a deluxe edition that matched the original's cover file). Give an album its cover with Set cover under the cover on its page (or Change cover to replace it), or pick an image right away in the Add album form (AVIF, JPEG, PNG or WebP; covers cannot be GIFs). An import only changes a cover when its inbox has a file for that album. An album page can also add all its songs to a playlist, or Copy the album under a new name (a deluxe edition, say) with its cover, songs, lyrics and song videos; the copy opens right away. The ‹ and › arrows step through the artist's albums, from the oldest to the newest, like the ones on a song page step through its album.
🎵 Songs
Every song, a page at a time, your favorites first (in the A–Z order). Search them by title, artist or album, sort them, or show only favorites, listen later or the ones missing lyrics. The Missing lyrics column marks the songs that still need theirs. Songs are edited on their album's page, not here. Open a song to read its lyrics, or press Open lyrics file to write them in your text editor; save the file and press Reload lyrics. Edit details on a song's page takes your notes, the song's music video and the other artists it features: they show as feat. next to the song's artist, and the song shows in the Songs featured section of their own pages. The Videos card below holds as many other videos as you like (lessons, covers, live takes), each with a title and where to start playing, which you can reorder. Paste YouTube links (youtube.com/watch?v=…, youtu.be/…, shorts, embeds) or just the 11-character video id; a t= in a link sets the start.
📜 Playlists
Make playlists, then add songs to them from a song's page, or all of an album's songs from the album's page (songs already in the playlist stay where they are). The Add songs form at the bottom of a playlist adds several at once: search, press + to list each song, then Add songs; Drop songs empties the list instead. On a playlist, move songs with ↑ and ↓, or take them out with Remove; they stay in your library. Favorites and Listen Later are built in and cannot be renamed or deleted: they always hold the songs marked ♥ and ⧗, and adding a song to them or taking it out sets or clears that mark.
🛒 Budget
A cart of the CDs and vinyls you plan to buy, and what they cost together. Pick the band (your favorites come first), then the album (split into Wanted, Neither and Owned), then the format, and type its price (24.99 or 24,99), how many copies, and if you like a link to it on a shop's website; the cart adds up the total, plus the Shipping and minus the optional Discount you type under the albums (0.00 until you do), both saved with the budget; a discount can take the total down to 0.00, no further. Albums you already own on that format can go in too, but each one is flagged ⚠ You already own this. − and + change how many copies; − on the last copy asks before taking it out. Under the cart, Make Budget saves it under the name you give and empties it, Buy marks every album in it owned on its format and keeps it as a bought budget, and Delete empties it. Saved budgets are listed below with their totals: Buy one when you got its albums, Edit it to load it into the cart (press Save Budget there to keep the changes, or Delete to drop them), or Delete it. Bought budgets stay as a record of what you spent. When you buy albums that other budgets still to buy also hold (on the same format), you are asked first whether to drop them from those budgets; any budget that leaves empty is deleted. Prices show with a generic $, whatever currency the shops use.
🎟️ Concerts
The concerts listed in local/concerts.csv, next to the library (columns date;band;tour;country;city;Want to go;Going), upcoming ones first, with the band's picture. Set each one to Want to go or Going, and once it is over tick its Went box; that is saved in the file. Add concert adds one to the file, and Add tour keeps a band's tour so Add concert offers it. The copy button at the end of a row fills Add concert with that concert: change what you need and add it. A concert can also have a Setlist: pick one of your playlists, holding the songs played there, and it is linked from the concert (and the playlist page says which concerts it is the setlist of). The file keeps the playlist's name in a Setlist column, and renaming the playlist renames it there too. The file is read every time the page opens, so you can also edit it and reload.
🔴 Live Play
Press Live Play on an album or playlist to follow it as you listen: the cover, the song and its length, the artist, a ♡ and a ⧖ for the song, and its lyrics. ‹ and › go to the previous and next song, the middle button pauses and resumes the song, and ↺ plays it again. Every song that comes on logs a listen, and when its length has gone by the next one comes on by itself, unless it is paused. Reloading the page or the lyrics logs nothing. A song with a length and a timed lyrics file (.lrc) lights up its lines as they go by. implicated plays no sound, so its clock starts when the song comes on here: click a line as you hear it to catch up with your music.
👥 Duo
Swap data files with a friend: Download my data gives yours, and Add friend takes theirs, which is kept on its own and never goes into your library. Pick a friend to see your taste match and what you have in common, and recommendations: what you both love, what you both want to listen to later, what you both play on repeat, your listen later records they love (move them up!), what they recommend from your library and play a lot that you never played, what you should share with them, artists of theirs to discover, and the CDs and vinyls one of you owns and the other wants. Sections with songs can become a playlist with one click. Loving means a favorite or 4 stars or more.
The same page imports your own file back, say on another computer. Artists, albums and songs are matched by name: whatever the library does not have is skipped and listed. The records in the file take its marks and values, plays are added (never twice) and playlists are created or replaced by name.
📥 Import
Bring in a batch of files from the inbox (info/): see what they would change first, then Accept. Accepted files move to an archive, so the inbox is empty again; delete archives there once you no longer need them (you are asked first). The page also downloads a backup or an export of your library (see Backups and exports).
🚪 Exit
The door at the top right closes implicated: confirm, and the window closes (if the browser lets it) and the server stops. Everything is saved as you go.

✨ Everyday actions

♡ Favorite
Mark an artist, album or song. Favorites are listed first and on the overview.
★ Rating
Click the stars of an artist, album or song to rate it from 1 to 5; click its current rating again to take it away.
⧖ Listen later
Mark an artist, album or song to listen to later; the overview lists them, and the Albums and Songs pages can show only them. A song loses its mark once you log a listen of it.
▶ Log a listen
Records that you played a song right now; it feeds the play charts and history. Song pages have a ▶ Played button too, and on an album page it logs a listen of every song on the album.
↕️ Sorting
Click a column header of a table to sort by it; click it again to reverse the order. On the paged Songs list this sorts the page in view; the order selector above it sorts all of them.
✏️ Edit and delete
Buttons on each artist, album and song. Deleting an artist also deletes their albums, songs and plays.
⏱️ Song lengths
Type them as 3:45 or as plain seconds.

📥 Importing from files

🗄️ The library is the one source of truth. Small edits (a year, a rating, a new song) belong in the app. For bigger batches, like an artist's whole discography or a list of concerts, prepare files in the inbox, info/, and import them from the Import page (or make import). Nothing is imported on its own, not even when the server starts, so what you edit in the app always stays.

The inbox can hold a bands.yaml (artists, origin, genres, languages, a description, a Wikipedia link and members), plus bands/<artist>/albums.yaml (albums and their songs), bands/<artist>/band.jpg (a picture of the artist), bands/<artist>/covers/ (cover images, matched to albums by file name), bands/<artist>/songs/<song title>.txt (lyrics; a / in the title becomes _, and <>:"\|?* are left out), optional <song title>.lrc timed lyrics (a timestamp per line, like [01:23.40]Words) and bands/<artist>/tabs/ Guitar Pro files. A concerts.csv and tours.csv are added to yours, and a Download my data file named userdata.json brings back your marks.

🧩 Records are matched by their uid (exports carry one, so renamed records are still found) or else by name. A key in a file wins; a key left out keeps what you have, so a file only needs what it changes. Ratings, notes, favorites, CD and vinyl ownership, videos, listen later marks, playlists and plays are never touched by the YAML. Lyrics, timed lyrics and tabs are copied into the library's own folder (local/bands/), where the song page's buttons open them; an empty lyrics file in the inbox changes nothing. A broken file stops the whole import, and nothing changes.

📦 Backups and exports

Everything the app keeps lives in one folder, local/: the database, covers, artist pictures, lyrics, tabs, concerts and friends' files.

  • Backup (download or make backup): that folder, exactly, as a zip. make restore FILE=…, with the app stopped, puts it back, after zipping what was there.
  • Export (download or make export): your library written out as an inbox, in YAML with a uid on every record, plus the files and your data. Upload it on the Import page (here or in a fresh library) to rebuild the library from it, or unzip it to edit the files by hand. Songs without an album cannot be written to albums.yaml and are left out.

🔌 API
