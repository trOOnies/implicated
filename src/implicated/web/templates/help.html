Help

What is implicated?

A personal library of the artists, albums and songs you like. Everything you add is part of the library straight away; listening to it is optional.

Pages

Overview
Totals, latest releases and charts of your library. Play history and favorites show up here once you have some.
Artists
Every artist with their genres. Open one to see its line-up, discography and songs, edit it, or add albums and songs.
Albums
All albums with their covers. Open one for its tracklist, rating and notes.
Songs
Search every song by title, artist or album, sort them, or show only favorites.

Everyday actions

♡ Favorite
Mark an artist, album or song. Favorites are listed first and on the overview.
▶ Log a listen
Records that you played a song right now; it feeds the play charts and history.
Edit and delete
Buttons on each artist, album and song. Deleting an artist also deletes their albums, songs and plays.
Song lengths
Type them as 3:45 or as plain seconds.

Importing from files

Put a bands.yaml in the info/ folder (artists, genres, languages, a description, a Wikipedia link and members), plus bands/<artist>/albums.yaml (albums and their songs), bands/<artist>/band.jpg (a picture of the artist) and bands/<artist>/covers/ (cover images, matched to albums by file name). The server imports the folder every time it starts, so restart it after editing the files. Importing again updates what changed and keeps your ratings, notes, favorites and plays. Problems are listed in the server log.

API

Everything is also available as JSON under /api. The API page lists every route and lets you try them in the browser.
