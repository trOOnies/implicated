Help

What is implicated?

A personal library of the artists, albums and songs you like. Everything you add is part of the library straight away; listening to it is optional.

Pages

Overview
Totals, latest releases and charts of your library. Play history and favorites show up here once you have some.
Artists
Every artist with their genres. Open one to see its line-up, discography and songs, edit it (also to write your own notes, which imports leave alone), or add albums and songs.
Albums
All albums with their covers and how many of their songs have lyrics. Open one for its tracklist, rating and notes; songs without lyrics are marked missing lyrics, and songs with no words to have (tick No lyrics when adding or editing them) no lyrics. Those do not count against the album's lyrics percentage. The CD and Vinyl selectors on an album page (and in an artist's discography) say whether you want or own it on each format; filter the albums by Wanted (CD), Owned (CD), Wanted (Vinyl) or Owned (Vinyl), favorites or listen later.
Songs
Search every song by title, artist or album, sort them, or show only favorites. The Missing lyrics column marks the songs that still need theirs. Open a song to read its lyrics, or press Open lyrics file to write them in your text editor; save the file and press Reload lyrics. Edit notes & MV on a song's page takes your notes and the song's music video: paste its YouTube link (youtube.com/watch?v=…, youtu.be/…, shorts, embeds) or just its 11-character id, and the page plays it.

Everyday actions

♡ Favorite
Mark an artist, album or song. Favorites are listed first and on the overview.
⧖ Listen later
Mark an artist, album or song to listen to later; the overview lists them, and the Albums and Songs pages can show only them. A song loses its mark once you log a listen of it.
▶ Log a listen
Records that you played a song right now; it feeds the play charts and history. Song pages have a ▶ Played button too.
Sorting
Click a column header of a table to sort by it; click it again to reverse the order.
Edit and delete
Buttons on each artist, album and song. Deleting an artist also deletes their albums, songs and plays.
Song lengths
Type them as 3:45 or as plain seconds.

Importing from files

Put a bands.yaml in the info/ folder (artists, origin, genres, languages, a description, a Wikipedia link and members), plus bands/<artist>/albums.yaml (albums and their songs), bands/<artist>/band.jpg (a picture of the artist) and bands/<artist>/covers/ (cover images, matched to albums by file name) and bands/<artist>/songs/<song title>.txt (lyrics; a / in the title becomes _, and <>:"\|?* are left out). Every song gets one: songs you add without a file get an empty one. The server imports the folder every time it starts, so restart it after editing the files. Importing again updates what changed and keeps your ratings, notes, favorites, CD and vinyl ownership, music videos, listen later marks and plays. Problems are listed in the server log.

API

Everything is also available as JSON under /api. The API page lists every route, grouped by what it works on (artists, members, albums, songs, plays and stats), and lets you try them in the browser.
