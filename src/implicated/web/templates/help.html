Help

🎧 What is implicated?

A personal library of the artists, albums and songs you like. Everything you add is part of the library straight away; listening to it is optional.

🗺️ Pages

📊 Overview
Totals, latest releases and charts of your library. Play history and favorites show up here once you have some.
🎤 Artists
Every artist with their genres; filter them by name, origin, genre or language. Open one to see its line-up, discography and songs, edit it (also to write your own notes, which imports leave alone), or add albums and songs.
💿 Albums
All albums with their covers and how many of their songs have lyrics. Open one for its tracklist, rating and notes; songs without lyrics are marked missing lyrics, and songs with no words to have (tick No lyrics when adding or editing them) no lyrics. Those do not count against the album's lyrics percentage. The CD and Vinyl selectors on an album page (and in an artist's discography) say whether you want or own it on each format; filter the albums by Wanted (CD), Owned (CD), Wanted (Vinyl) or Owned (Vinyl), favorites, listen later, the ones with songs still missing lyrics, or the ones with no own cover: albums marked no cover (the ♪ placeholder shows instead) or shared cover (the same image as another album, like a deluxe edition that matched the original's cover file). An album page can also add all its songs to a playlist, or Copy the album under a new name (a deluxe edition, say) with its cover, songs, lyrics and song videos; the copy opens right away.
🎵 Songs
Search every song by title, artist or album, sort them, or show only favorites, listen later or the ones missing lyrics. The Missing lyrics column marks the songs that still need theirs. Open a song to read its lyrics, or press Open lyrics file to write them in your text editor; save the file and press Reload lyrics. Edit notes & MV on a song's page takes your notes and the song's music video. The Videos card below holds as many other videos as you like (lessons, covers, live takes), each with a title and where to start playing, which you can reorder. Paste YouTube links (youtube.com/watch?v=…, youtu.be/…, shorts, embeds) or just the 11-character video id; a t= in a link sets the start.
📜 Playlists
Make playlists, then add songs to them from a song's page, or all of an album's songs from the album's page (songs already in the playlist stay where they are). On a playlist, move songs with ↑ and ↓, or take them out with Remove; they stay in your library. Favorites and Listen Later are built in and cannot be renamed or deleted: they always hold the songs marked ♥ and ⧗, and adding a song to them or taking it out sets or clears that mark.
🛒 Budget
A cart of the CDs and vinyls you plan to buy, and what they cost together. Pick the format, then the album (the ones you marked Wanted on that format come first), and type its price (24.99 or 24,99), how many copies, and if you like a link to it on a shop's website; the cart adds up the total. Albums you already own on that format can go in too, but each one is flagged ⚠ You already own this. − and + change how many copies; − on the last copy asks before taking it out. Under the cart, Make Budget saves it under the name you give and empties it, Buy marks every album in it owned on its format and keeps it as a bought budget, and Delete empties it. Saved budgets are listed below with their totals: Buy one when you got its albums, Edit it to load it into the cart (press Save Budget there to keep the changes, or Delete to drop them), or Delete it. Bought budgets stay as a record of what you spent. When you buy albums that other budgets still to buy also hold (on the same format), you are asked first whether to drop them from those budgets; any budget that leaves empty is deleted.

✨ Everyday actions

♡ Favorite
Mark an artist, album or song. Favorites are listed first and on the overview.
★ Rating
Click the stars of an artist, album or song to rate it from 1 to 5; click its current rating again to take it away.
⧖ Listen later
Mark an artist, album or song to listen to later; the overview lists them, and the Albums and Songs pages can show only them. A song loses its mark once you log a listen of it.
▶ Log a listen
Records that you played a song right now; it feeds the play charts and history. Song pages have a ▶ Played button too.
↕️ Sorting
Click a column header of a table to sort by it; click it again to reverse the order.
✏️ Edit and delete
Buttons on each artist, album and song. Deleting an artist also deletes their albums, songs and plays.
⏱️ Song lengths
Type them as 3:45 or as plain seconds.

📥 Importing from files

Put a bands.yaml in the info/ folder (artists, origin, genres, languages, a description, a Wikipedia link and members), plus bands/<artist>/albums.yaml (albums and their songs), bands/<artist>/band.jpg (a picture of the artist) and bands/<artist>/covers/ (cover images, matched to albums by file name) and bands/<artist>/songs/<song title>.txt (lyrics; a / in the title becomes _, and <>:"\|?* are left out). Every song gets one: songs you add without a file get an empty one.

🔄 The server imports the folder every time it starts, so restart it after editing the files. Importing again updates what changed and keeps your ratings, notes, favorites, CD and vinyl ownership, music videos, song videos, listen later marks, playlists and plays. Problems are listed in the server log.

🔌 API

Everything is also available as JSON under /api. The API page lists every route, grouped by what it works on (artists, members, albums, songs, song videos, playlists, plays and stats), and lets you try them in the browser.
