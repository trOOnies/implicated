Drag the songs of {{ tier_list.name }} into the levels, and between them. To move many at once, select them: click each one (or press Enter on it), Shift+click to select every song between two, or drag a box around them from an empty spot. Then drag one of them, or click a level (or press Enter on it) to put them last there. Escape clears the selection. Click a level's name to rename it. In locking mode, the lock on a song (shown when you point at it) locks it where it is, as decided (its cover and lock go grey): it can't be moved until unlocked, though other songs can go anywhere around it. A selection is of unlocked songs (gold) or of locked ones (grey), never both: the lock on any of them locks, or unlocks, them all. Turn locking mode off to move every song freely; on again, your locked songs are locked where they are then. Make playlist turns the ranking into a playlist: pick the last level to take, and it holds the songs from the top level down to that one, in order. Fast assign places the unassigned songs one at a time, by asking you which of two songs is better until each finds its place. In locking mode, a level whose songs are all locked (or that has none) goes grey.
