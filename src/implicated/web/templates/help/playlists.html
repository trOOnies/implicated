Lists of songs in the order you choose. Open Playlists from the top bar.

➕ Making a playlist

Name it in New playlist and press Create playlist.

🎶 Adding songs

  • From a song's page, one at a time.
  • From an album's page, all of its songs. Songs already in the playlist stay where they are.
  • With the Add songs form at the bottom of a playlist, several at once:
  1. Search for a song and press + to list it.
  2. Repeat for each song.
  3. Press Add songs, or Drop songs to empty the list instead.

↕️ Arranging

On a playlist, move songs with ↑ and ↓, or take them out with Remove. They stay in your library.

💡 For a ranking of a playlist's songs, make it a tier list. A playlist can also be a concert's setlist.

⭐ Favorites and Listen Later

These two are built in, and cannot be renamed or deleted.

  • They always hold the songs marked ♥ and ⧗.
  • Adding a song to them, or taking it out, sets or clears that mark.

📑 Copying a playlist

  1. Press the copy button by a playlist: New playlist turns into Copy playlist, naming the one being copied.
  2. Name the copy and press Create playlist. It gets the same songs, in the same order.

Cancel turns it back into New playlist. A copy of Favorites or Listen Later is a playlist of your own, which keeps its songs whatever you mark later.
