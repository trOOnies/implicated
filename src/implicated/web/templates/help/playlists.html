Lists of songs in the order you choose. Open Playlists from the top bar.

➕ Making a playlist

Name it in New playlist, pick its Folder and press Create playlist.

📁 Folders

Folders sort your playlists. Every playlist is in exactly one.

FolderWhat is in it
DefaultFavorites, Listen Later, and any playlist you put in no other folder.
SetlistsExactly the playlists that are a concert's setlist.
Your ownWhatever you put in them.
  • Make one with New folder; rename or delete it beside its name. Its playlists go to Default when you delete it.
  • Move a playlist with the Folder menu in its row, or on its page.
  • Default and Setlists are built in: they cannot be renamed or deleted.

💡 You never move a playlist into or out of Setlists yourself. Pick it as a concert's setlist and it goes in; take it off every concert and it goes back to Default.

🖼️ Cover

Press Set cover on a playlist's page to give it an image of its own: square, up to 2 MB, as AVIF, JPG, PNG or WebP. Remove cover goes back to the default one.

Without a cover of its ownIt shows
A setlistThe band's picture
Any other playlist with songsThe cover of the album it has the most songs of (on a tie, the one whose song comes first)
No songsThe ♪ placeholder

🎶 Adding songs

  • From a song's page, one at a time.
  • From an album's page, all of its songs. Songs already in the playlist stay where they are.
  • With the Add songs form at the bottom of a playlist, several at once:
  1. Search for a song and press + to list it.
  2. Repeat for each song.
  3. Press Add songs, or Drop songs to empty the list instead.

🔎 Search filters

Type a filter before or after the words you search, ending it with a space: it turns into a tag in the box. Press ✕ on a tag to remove it, or Backspace at the start of the box to edit the last one.

artist:abbaSongs by an artist whose name has “abba” in it
album:arrivalSongs on an album whose title has “arrival”
from:1975 to:1979Songs on an album from that year on, or up to it
language:spanishSongs by an artist who sings in that language
genre:popSongs by an artist of that genre
stars:5Songs rated that many stars, 1 to 5
fav:Favorite songs (♥)
later:Songs to listen to later (⧗)

Put a value with spaces in quotes, like artist:"the cure". Filters alone are a search too: fav: stars:5 lists your five-star favorites.

↕️ Arranging

On a playlist, drag a song by the grip ⠿ at its left to move it, or take it out with Remove; it stays in your library.

  • Click a grip to select its song, Ctrl + click (⌘ on a Mac) to add or drop one, and Shift + click to select every song from the last one clicked.
  • Drag any selected grip to move all the selected songs together. Escape clears the selection.
  • With the keyboard: ↑ and ↓ go from grip to grip (Shift selects as you go), and Alt + ↑ or Alt + ↓ moves the selection one place.

💡 Dragging works in the playlist's own order. If you sorted the table by another column, sort it by # to get that order back.

💡 For a ranking of a playlist's songs, make it a tier list. A playlist can also be a concert's setlist.

⭐ Favorites and Listen Later

These two are built in, and cannot be renamed or deleted.

  • They always hold the songs marked ♥ and ⧗.
  • Adding a song to them, or taking it out, sets or clears that mark.

📑 Copying a playlist

  1. Press the copy button by a playlist: New playlist turns into Copy playlist, naming the one being copied.
  2. Name the copy and press Create playlist. It gets the same songs, in the same order.

Cancel turns it back into New playlist. A copy of Favorites or Listen Later is a playlist of your own, which keeps its songs whatever you mark later.
