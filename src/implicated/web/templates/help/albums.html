All albums, with their covers and how many of their songs have lyrics. Open Albums from the top bar.

🔎 Finding albums

They come a page at a time, your favorites first (the ♡ on a cover marks one), or Newest first. Show only:

  • Wanted (CD), Owned (CD), Wanted (Vinyl) or Owned (Vinyl);
  • favorites, or listen later;
  • the ones with songs still missing lyrics;
  • the ones with no own cover (see Covers).

📄 An album's page

  • Its tracklist, rating and notes.
  • ▶ Played logs a listen of every song on it.
  • Add all its songs to a playlist.
  • Follow it in Live Play, or rank it in a tier list.
  • The ‹ and › arrows step through the artist's albums, oldest to newest.

📝 Lyrics and explicit songs

Songs are tagged in the tracklist:

  • missing lyrics: lyrics still to add.
  • no lyrics: nothing to add (tick No lyrics when adding or editing the song). These don't count against the album's lyrics percentage.
  • E by the title: explicit content, in tracklists, on the song's page and in tier lists. Tick Explicit when adding or editing a song, or on its page's Edit details.

💡 New or changed lyrics with obvious swearing tick Explicit on their own. Mild words like damn or hell don't count; untick it by hand if a song gets it wrong.

💽 CD and vinyl

The CD and Vinyl selectors on an album page, and in an artist's discography, say whether you want or own it on each format. The Budget uses them too.

🖼️ Covers

  • Set cover under the cover on its page (or Change cover) gives the album its cover. The Add album form takes one right away too.
  • Covers can be AVIF, JPEG, PNG or WebP, not GIFs.
  • no cover: the ♪ placeholder shows instead.
  • shared cover: the same image as another album, like a deluxe edition that matched the original's cover file.
  • An import only changes a cover when its inbox has a file for that album.

📑 Copying an album

Copy on an album page makes it again under a new name (a deluxe edition, say), with its cover, songs, lyrics and song videos. The copy opens right away.

🔗 Editions

Two editions of an album can count as one. In the Editions card of an album page:

  1. Pick any of the artist's albums; the names needn't match.
  2. Choose whether this one goes Into that one, or that one Into this one. The one merged into is the main edition.
  3. Their songs of the same name (ignoring case and kinds of dash) are merged too, the main edition's being the main versions (see Same song).
  4. The card lists the songs left unmerged: pick by hand which song of the other editions each is (say, "Tim McGraw (Taylor's Version)" and "Tim McGraw").
  • The editions share the ♡ and ⧖ marks and the rating, and the main one stands for them on the Overview.
  • Unmerge on an edition also unmerges its songs from the other editions'.
  • Deleting the main edition makes the next one the main one.
