Put your taste next to a friend's. Open Duo from the top bar.

🔁 Swapping files

  • Download my data gives your file, to send to your friend.
  • Add friend takes theirs. It is kept on its own, and never goes into your library.

🤝 What you have in common

Pick a friend to see your taste match, and recommendations:

  • what you both love, both want to listen to later, and both play on repeat;
  • your listen later records they love (move them up!);
  • what they recommend from your library and play a lot, that you never played;
  • what you should share with them, and artists of theirs to discover;
  • the CDs and vinyls one of you owns and the other wants.

Loving means a favorite, or 4 stars or more. Sections with songs can become a playlist with one click.

📥 Your own file

The same page imports your own file back, say on another computer.

  • Artists, albums and songs are matched by name; whatever the library does not have is skipped and listed.
  • The records in the file take its marks and values.
  • Plays are added, never twice.
  • Playlists are created, or replaced by name.
