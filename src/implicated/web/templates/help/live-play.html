Follow an album or a playlist as you listen to it, with each song's lyrics.

▶️ Starting

Press Live Play on an album or a playlist. It shows the cover, the song and its length, the artist, a ♡ and a ⧖ for the song, and its lyrics.

🎛️ The controls

  • ‹ and › go to the previous and next song.
  • The middle button pauses and resumes the song.
  • ↺ plays it again.

Every song that comes on logs a listen. When its length has gone by, the next one comes on by itself, unless it is paused. Reloading the page or the lyrics logs nothing.

🎤 Timed lyrics

A song with a length and a timed lyrics file (.lrc) lights up its lines as they go by.

💡 implicated plays no sound, so its clock starts when the song comes on here. Click a line as you hear it to catch up with your music.
