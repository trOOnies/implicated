Everything the app keeps lives in one folder, local/: the database, covers, artist pictures, the pictures you saved of them, lyrics, tabs, concerts and friends' files.

🗃️ Backup

Download it, or run make backup: that folder, exactly, as a zip.

To put it back, stop the app and run make restore FILE=…. It zips what was there first.

📤 Export

Download it, or run make export: your library written out as an inbox, in YAML with a uid on every record, plus the files and your data.

  • Upload it on the Import page, here or in a fresh library, to rebuild the library from it.
  • Or unzip it to edit the files by hand.

⚠️ Songs without an album cannot be written to albums.yaml and are left out, and so are the pictures you saved of artists. Only a backup keeps those.
