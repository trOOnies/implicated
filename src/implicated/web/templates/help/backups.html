Everything the app keeps lives in one folder, local/: the database, covers, artist pictures, the pictures you saved of them, lyrics, tabs, concerts and friends' files.

  • Backup (download or make backup): that folder, exactly, as a zip. make restore FILE=…, with the app stopped, puts it back, after zipping what was there.
  • Export (download or make export): your library written out as an inbox, in YAML with a uid on every record, plus the files and your data. Upload it on the Import page (here or in a fresh library) to rebuild the library from it, or unzip it to edit the files by hand. Songs without an album cannot be written to albums.yaml and are left out, and so are the pictures you saved of artists: only a backup keeps those.
