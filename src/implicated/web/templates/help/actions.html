Buttons you'll find on artists, albums and songs alike.

♡ Favorite

Mark an artist, album or song. Favorites are listed first and on the Overview.

★ Rating

Click the stars of an artist, album or song to rate it from 1 to 5. Click its current rating again to take it away.

⧖ Listen later

Mark an artist, album or song to listen to later. The Overview lists them, and the Artists, Albums and Songs pages can show only them. The mark goes away on its own:

  • a song: once you log a listen of it, however you do it;
  • an album: when you log a listen of the whole album (its ▶ Played button), or Live Play reaches its last song;
  • an artist: never; take it off yourself.

▶ Log a listen

Records that you played a song right now. It feeds the play charts and history.

  • Song pages have a ▶ Played button too.
  • On an album page, it logs a listen of every song on the album.
  • Live Play logs each song as it comes on.

↕️ Sorting

Click a column header of a table to sort by it; click it again to reverse the order.

💡 On the Songs page this sorts the page in view. The order selector above the list sorts all of them.

✏️ Edit and delete

Each artist, album and song has its buttons.

⚠️ Deleting an artist also deletes their albums, songs and plays.

⏱️ Song lengths

Type them as 3:45, or as plain seconds.
