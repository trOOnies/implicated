Every artist, with their genres, over a grey picture of them when they have one. Open Artists from the top bar.

🔎 Finding artists

They come a page at a time, favorites first. Narrow them down by:

  • name, origin, genre or language;
  • only Favorites, or only Listen later.

➕ Adding an artist

The Add artist form can mark a new artist as a favorite or to listen later, and rate them, right away. The year they formed cannot be in the future.

💡 On a narrow window, the form is below the list.

📄 An artist's page

  • Their line-up and discography.
  • Edit them, also to write your own notes, which imports leave alone.
  • Add albums to them.
  • Songs featured: other artists' songs they appear in (see a song's details).
  • A tier list of all their songs.

🖼️ Pictures

The Pictures section of an artist's page shows the newest pictures you saved of them, and leads to all of them, a page of thumbnails at a time.

  • Add one: any image, GIFs too, with a name, a description and tags, all optional.
  • Search them by name, file name, description or tag, or pick a tag to see only those.
  • Open one to see it big, with its name, the file it came from, its description and tags (click a tag for the other pictures with it), and to edit or delete it.

👤 Profile picture

  • Set picture (or Change picture) under the artist's picture uploads a new one.
  • It is kept in their Pictures too, named Profile picture and tagged profile.
  • Any square picture of theirs can take its place: open it and press Make profile picture. The current one says Profile picture instead.
