Rank the songs of any playlist, album or artist in levels, from S down.

🏁 Starting one

  1. Press Tier List on a playlist's, album's or artist's page.
  2. Press Make a tier list.
  • It starts with the levels S, A, B, C and D, and every song below them, Unassigned.
  • An album's songs come in track order; an artist's are all of their own, album by album.
  • Each song shows as its album cover with its title on top. Point at it for its artist, album and plays.
  • Each playlist, album and artist has its own: a song can be S on its album and C on a playlist.

✋ Moving songs

Drag songs into the levels and between them. To move many at once, select them first:

Select a songClick it, or press Enter or Space on it
Select a rangeShift+click
Select by areaDrag a box from an empty spot
Select every unassigned songSelect all
Move the selectionDrag one of them, or click a level (or press Enter on it) to put them last there
Clear the selectionEscape, or Clear

📶 Levels

  • + Add Tier, the dotted row below the last level, adds one at the bottom, named after the next free letter.
  • Click a level's name (or ✎) to rename it, up to 32 characters.
  • ✕ deletes it; its songs go back to the unassigned ones.
  • A tier list has 3 to 10 levels; the count is under the levels.

➕ Autoadd

After asking, it empties the levels and fills the first three, each by plays, most first:

  1. the favorite songs;
  2. the songs on a favorite album;
  3. the other songs played at least once.

The rest stay unassigned. A tier list of 2 levels gets a third one.

🔒 Locking mode

On in a new tier list, it lets you lock a song where it is, as decided: press the lock on it, shown when you point at it.

  • A locked song's cover and lock grey out, and so does a level's name once every song in it is locked (or it has none).
  • It can't be dragged, or moved by Autoadd, until you unlock it. Other songs can still go anywhere around it.
  • A selection holds unlocked songs (in gold) or locked ones (in grey), never both. Selecting a song of the other kind starts a new selection.
  • The lock on any selected song, or Lock and Unlock by the selection, lock or unlock all of them.
  • Turning locking mode off frees every song; turning it on again locks the same songs, wherever they are by then. The box shows how many are locked.

⚡ Fast assign

Places the unassigned (unlocked) songs one by one, asking you which of two songs is better. It searches like a binary search: first against the levels' top and bottom songs to find the song's level, then within that level.

It first asks whether you will listen to each song as you rank it. If so, each song logs a listen the first time it comes up, once, whatever you undo.

Left song is betterClick it, or A or ←
Right song is betterClick it, or D or →
Not sureidk, or S or ↓
Take back the last answerUndo, or Backspace
Leave the song unassignedSkip song
Close itEscape

idk asks about another song close by first. It works while another song can still decide the place; the last one that can has to be answered.

When a song falls between two levels, a last question asks which border it is: the bottom of the upper level or the top of the lower one. Empty levels between them are options too: move between them with A/← and D/→, and press Enter (or click one). With no song to place, it only warns you.

📜 Make playlist

Makes a new playlist of the ranked songs, in order, from the top level down to the one you pick (that one included). It is named after what is ranked, like Dummy (Tier list); rename it on its page.

It is a copy of the songs as they are then, not linked to the tier list: changing or deleting the tier list leaves it as it is, and making another one makes another playlist.

💡 A song that leaves the playlist, album or artist leaves its tier list too, and Delete tier list leaves the songs as they were.
