Every song, a page at a time. Open Songs from the top bar.

🔎 Finding songs

  • Your favorites come first, in A–Z order; the order selector sorts them otherwise.
  • Search them by title, artist or album.
  • Show only favorites, listen later, or the ones missing lyrics (the Missing lyrics column marks them).
  • On a song's page, the ‹ and › arrows step through its album.

💡 Songs are added and edited on their album's page, not here.

📝 Lyrics

Open a song to read its lyrics. To write them:

  1. Press Open lyrics file: it opens in your text editor.
  2. Write and save the file.
  3. Press Reload lyrics.

Timed lyrics (.lrc) come in by importing and light up in Live Play.

✏️ Details

Edit details on a song's page takes:

  • your notes;
  • the song's music video;
  • the other artists it features: search for each one and pick it to add it as a tag (✕ takes it away). They show as feat. next to the song's artist, and the song shows in the Songs featured section of their own pages;
  • whether it is explicit.

🎬 Videos

The Videos card holds as many other videos as you like (lessons, covers, live takes), each with a title and where to start playing. Reorder them as you like.

Paste a YouTube link (youtube.com/watch?v=…, youtu.be/…, shorts, embeds) or just the 11-character video id. A t= in a link sets the start.

🔗 Same song

The same song on two albums (say, a deluxe edition and the original) can be merged, to count as one.

  • Each stays on its own album, with its own lyrics, tab, videos and notes.
  • Their plays add up, on every version's play count.
  • The ♥ and ⧗ marks and the rating are shared: marking one marks them all.
  • One is the main version: only it shows in Favorites, Listen Later, the Overview and tier lists. The others are tagged merged in song lists.

The Same song card of a song's page suggests the artist's songs with the same title, finds any other song to merge, and lets you Make main another version or Unmerge one. Deleting the main version makes the next one the main one.

💡 Merging two editions of an album merges their songs for you.
