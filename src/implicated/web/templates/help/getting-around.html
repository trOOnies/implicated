Each tab along the top bar is a page of its own. The ❔ by a heading leads to the Help about it.

📊 Overview

Click the implicated logo at the top left to come here. It has a showcase of your library, and charts of it:

  • songs per artist, genres, albums by decade and album ratings;
  • plays in the last 30 days, once you have some.

🖼️ The showcase

A grid of up to 30 pictures. Pick its Type and its Order:

  • See more... opens the rest on the Artists, Albums or Songs page, already filtered, when that page can show them.

❔ The Help icon

The quiet ? beside a heading opens this Help at the part about it.

🌙 Dark mode

implicated starts in dark mode. The moon at the top right, left of Exit, switches to light mode, and the sun switches back. The browser remembers your pick.

🚪 Exit

The door at the top right closes implicated: confirm, and the window closes (if the browser lets it) and the server stops.

💡 Everything is saved as you go, so there is nothing to save before leaving.
