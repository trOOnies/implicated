A personal library of the artists, albums and songs you like. Everything you add is part of the library straight away; listening to it is optional.

It's a tracker of your listening habits and likes, not a music player or a music store.

  • implicated does not host, upload or ship any copyrighted material: the artists, albums, songs, covers, lyrics and tabs in it are the ones you provide, from your own sources.
  • It does not play your music: listen in the apps and services where you have lawful access to it, then log your listens here.
  • Videos you link play in YouTube's own embedded player, and the tab player synthesizes the notes of a tab file, not a recording.
