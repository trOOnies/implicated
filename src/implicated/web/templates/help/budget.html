A cart of the CDs and vinyls you plan to buy, and what they cost together. Open Budget from the top bar.

🛒 Filling the cart

  1. Pick the band; your favorites come first.
  2. Pick the album, split into Wanted, Neither and Owned.
  3. Pick the format.
  4. Type its price (24.99 or 24,99), how many copies, and if you like a link to it on a shop's website.
  • − and + change how many copies; − on the last copy asks before taking it out.
  • Albums you already own on that format can go in too, but they are flagged ⚠ You already own this.

🧮 The total

The cart adds up the albums, plus the Shipping and minus the optional Discount you type under them (0.00 until you do). Both are saved with the budget.

  • A discount can take the total down to 0.00, no further.
  • Prices show with a generic $, whatever currency the shops use.

💾 Saving and buying

Under the cart:

  • Make Budget saves it under the name you give, and empties the cart.
  • Buy marks every album in it owned on its format, and keeps it as a bought budget.
  • Delete empties the cart.

🗂️ Saved budgets

They are listed below the cart, with their totals. For each one:

  • Buy it when you got its albums.
  • Edit it to load it into the cart, then press Save Budget there to keep the changes, or Delete to drop them.
  • Delete it.

Bought budgets stay as a record of what you spent.

💡 When you buy albums that other budgets still hold on the same format, you are asked first whether to drop them from those budgets. A budget left empty is deleted.
