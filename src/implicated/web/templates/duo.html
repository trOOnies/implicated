    📤 Export

    Download your favorites, listen later marks, ratings, notes, CD and vinyl ownership, music videos, featured artists, song videos, playlists and listening history ({{ stats.plays }} play{{ 's' if stats.plays != 1 }}) as one JSON file. Give it to a friend for their Duo page, or keep it as a backup.

    Artists, albums and songs are named, not numbered, so the file can be imported into another library built from the same info/ folder.

    📥 Import

    Bring back your own file downloaded here. The records it names take its marks, ratings, notes and videos; plays are added to your history (never twice) and playlists are created, or replaced, by name. A friend's file goes in Add friend above instead.

    Artists, albums and songs your library does not have are skipped and listed. Import info/ first if they are missing.
